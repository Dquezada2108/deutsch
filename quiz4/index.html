damit oder um … zu?

The choice depends on one thing: do both clauses have the same subject? Start with Welche Konstruktion? to train that decision, then Sätze verbinden, where nobody tells you which one to use.

Regeln: damit / um … zu

Die Entscheidung in 3 Schritten

  1. Underline the subject of the main clause and the subject of the goal clause.
  2. Same subject (ich … ich, wir … wir, die Kinder … sie): um … zu. Delete the subject and the modal verb, put zu + infinitive at the end.
  3. Different subjects (ich … du, Verena … andere Reisende): damit. Keep the subject, delete wollen / sollen / möchten, conjugated verb at the end.

Adjektivsteigerung

Positiv, Komparativ, Superlativ. Formen asks for the comparative or superlative, Positiv finden goes backwards, and Im Satz adds als / wie and adjective endings.
